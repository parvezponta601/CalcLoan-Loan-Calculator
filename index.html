This Privacy Policy explains how the CalcLoan Android app (the "App") handles information. We designed the App to respect your privacy: it performs all calculations on your device and does not send any information anywhere.

1 Information we collect

CalcLoan does not collect, transmit, sell, rent or share any personal information. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or account details
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics, crash reports or behavioral data

The loan amount, interest rate and term you type into the App are used only to perform the calculation on your device.

2 Data stored on your device

For your convenience, the App saves a small amount of data locally on your device using Android's private app storage:

  • The last values you entered (loan amount, interest rate, term and its unit)
  • Your chosen theme (Dark, Light or System default)

This data never leaves your device. It is not accessible to us or to any third party. You can remove it at any time by using the Reset option in the App menu, by clearing the App's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the App.

3 Permissions

The App does not request any Android permissions. It does not have internet access and cannot read your files, contacts, location or other personal data.

4 Advertising, analytics and third parties

CalcLoan contains no advertising, no analytics tools, no tracking and no third-party services or SDKs that collect data. Because nothing is transmitted, no data is shared with any third party.

Google Play, the store you use to download the App, may process certain information about your installation according to Google's own privacy policy. That processing is separate from the App and outside our control.

5 Data security

Since the App keeps everything on your device and makes no network connections, there is no server or database holding your information. Locally saved values are stored in the App's private storage, which is protected by the Android operating system. Cloud backup of the App's data is disabled.

6 Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 13. Because we do not collect any personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7 Calculation disclaimer

Results shown in the App are estimates based on the standard EMI formula, assuming a fixed interest rate and equal monthly payments. They are provided for information only and are not financial, legal or tax advice. Actual loan terms, fees, charges and payments may differ. Please confirm details with your lender before making financial decisions. The App does not offer loans, credit or payment services.

8 Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. If you have questions about your privacy or about this policy, you are welcome to contact us.

9 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App adds new features. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If a future version of the App changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
